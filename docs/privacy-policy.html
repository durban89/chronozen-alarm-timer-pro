Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 17, 2025  ·  Last Updated: August 17, 2025

🔒
Your privacy is fully protected — we collect absolutely nothing.

ChronoZen operates 100% offline. No personal data, browsing history, or usage information is ever collected, transmitted, or shared with any third party. Everything stays on your device.

📋 Overview

ChronoZen is a browser extension for Google Chrome that provides alarm clock, focus countdown timer, and stopwatch functionality. This Privacy Policy describes how ChronoZen handles your information.

We are committed to full transparency: ChronoZen does not collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal data of any kind.

📦 Information We Collect

ChronoZen does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Browsing history, URLs visited, or web activity
  • Location data
  • Financial or payment information
  • Usage analytics or telemetry data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data transmitted externally

The only data within ChronoZen is what you explicitly create (alarms, timer settings, preferences), stored exclusively on your local device.

🔑 Permissions & Why We Need Them

ChronoZen requests the following minimal Chrome permissions, each strictly necessary for core functionality:

alarms

Required to schedule alarm events in the background so they trigger at the exact time you set, even when the popup is closed. No data is read from or written to any external source using this permission.

notifications

Required to display desktop alert notifications when an alarm fires, including Snooze and Dismiss action buttons. Notifications are generated locally and never routed through any external server.

storage

Required to save your alarms, preferences (clock format, default sound, volume), and timer settings locally via chrome.storage.local. This data never leaves your device.

🌐 Network & Third-Party Services

ChronoZen makes zero network requests. The extension:

  • Does not connect to any external server, API, or cloud service
  • Does not use Google Analytics, Firebase, or any tracking SDK
  • Does not display advertisements of any kind
  • Does not load any external scripts or stylesheets at runtime
  • Functions entirely offline after installation

All alarm sounds are synthesized locally using the Web Audio API — no audio files are downloaded from the internet.

💾 Local Data Storage

ChronoZen stores the following data exclusively in chrome.storage.local on your device:

  • Your alarm configurations (time, label, repeat schedule, sound type, snooze duration)
  • App settings (clock format, default sound, volume level, mute state)
  • Active timer and stopwatch state

You can delete all stored data at any time by removing the extension from Chrome. No data persists after uninstallation.

👶 Children's Privacy

ChronoZen does not collect any personal data from any user, including children under the age of 13. The extension is fully compliant with COPPA by virtue of collecting no data whatsoever.
